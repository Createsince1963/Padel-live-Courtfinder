# Business Case

## Problem

Players often need to search across multiple providers and websites to find suitable courts.

## Solution

SmartCourtFinder provides one place to discover courts, compare options and access bookings quickly.

## Target Users

- Padel Players
- Tennis Players
- Business Travelers
- Casual Players
- Frequent Players

## Benefits

### For Players

- Save time
- Compare prices
- Find nearby courts
- Access bookings faster

### For Clubs

- Increased visibility
- Additional traffic
- More bookings

## Revenue Options

### Free Version

- Court search
- Favorites
- Booking links

### Premium Version

- Advanced filters
- Smart recommendations
- Travel optimization

### Future
